# The Lion and the Mouse

**Premise:** A tiny mouse spares a sleeping lion a fright, the lion spares the mouse, and later the mouse frees the lion from a hunter's net.

Source: Aesop, "The Lion and the Mouse" (public domain). Audience: children 4 to 8. Total running time: 60 seconds.

## Scenes

### Scene 1 (6 s)
- **Shot:** Wide establishing shot, slow push in.
- **We see:** A golden savanna at noon. Under a big acacia tree, a lion sleeps in the shade, tail twitching.
- **Narration:** "Under the big tree, the King of the grass was fast asleep."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Soft wind in the grass, cicadas, a low snore.

### Scene 2 (7 s)
- **Shot:** Low ground level tracking shot, following the mouse.
- **We see:** Pip the mouse scurries through the grass with a seed in her arms, looking back over her shoulder, and runs straight up the lion's paw and onto his nose.
- **Narration:** "Little Pip was in a hurry. Too much of a hurry."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Tiny pattering feet, a playful plucked string.

### Scene 3 (7 s)
- **Shot:** Close up on the lion's face, then a quick tilt down to his paw.
- **We see:** The lion's eyes snap open. His big paw comes down and traps Pip by her tail.
- **Narration:** "One big eye opened. Then one big paw came down."
- **Dialogue:** Lion (rumbling): "Who woke me?"
- **Sound:** Music stops. A deep thump.

### Scene 4 (8 s)
- **Shot:** Over the shoulder from the lion, looking down at Pip.
- **We see:** Pip, tiny under the paw, holds up her seed like a gift and bows.
- **Narration:** none
- **Dialogue:** Pip: "Please let me go, great Lion! One day, I will help you too."
- **Sound:** A small brave flute note.

### Scene 5 (7 s)
- **Shot:** Medium two shot, gentle and warm.
- **We see:** The lion laughs so hard his mane shakes. He lifts his paw. Pip bows again and runs off into the grass.
- **Narration:** "The lion laughed. How could something so small ever help him? But he let her go."
- **Dialogue:** Lion: "Ha! Off you go, little one."
- **Sound:** A big warm laugh, music returns softly.

### Scene 6 (8 s)
- **Shot:** Wide shot, the camera drifts slowly to one side.
- **We see:** Days later, the lion walks through the trees and steps into a hidden rope net. It snaps up and holds him tight, swinging just above the ground.
- **Narration:** "A few days later, the lion stepped into a hunter's net."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** A rope creak and snap, then a long sad roar echoing through the trees.

### Scene 7 (6 s)
- **Shot:** Close up of Pip's ears, then a quick whip pan in the direction of the roar.
- **We see:** Pip, far away, hears the roar. Her ears turn. She drops her seed and runs.
- **Narration:** "Far away, a small pair of ears heard him."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** The echo of the roar, then fast pattering feet.

### Scene 8 (7 s)
- **Shot:** Extreme close up of Pip's teeth on a rope, intercut with the lion's hopeful eyes.
- **We see:** Pip climbs the net and chews through one rope, then another, then another. The net opens and the lion drops gently to the ground.
- **Narration:** "Nibble by nibble, rope by rope."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Quick nibbling sounds, a rising string melody, a soft thud as he lands.

### Scene 9 (4 s)
- **Shot:** Low angle two shot at sunset, the camera rises slowly.
- **We see:** The lion lowers his huge head to Pip. She stands proudly on his nose. They smile at each other as the sun goes down.
- **Narration:** "Even the smallest friend can be the biggest help."
- **Dialogue:** none
- **Sound:** Warm closing chord, evening birds.

Scene durations: 6 + 7 + 7 + 8 + 7 + 8 + 6 + 7 + 4 = 60 seconds.

## Characters

**Leo the Lion**
- Look: big, soft golden lion with a fluffy, slightly messy mane, round friendly eyes and a pink nose; large, but never scary.
- Voice: deep, slow and rumbling, with a big warm laugh.
- Manner: proud and sleepy at first, then grateful and gentle.

**Pip the Mouse**
- Look: tiny brown field mouse with oversized round ears, a white tummy and a long thin tail; carries a sunflower seed.
- Voice: small, quick and bright, brave even when scared.
- Manner: always in a hurry, polite, keeps her promise.

**The Hunter (only his net is seen)**
- Look: never shown; only a brown rope net hanging from a tree branch.
- Voice: none.
- Manner: a quiet danger, kept off screen so the film stays gentle for young children.
